## 📌 Overview

Exploratory data analysis (EDA) of customer purchase data for **Dibs**, a rapidly expanding retail company operating across multiple shopping malls. The goal was to uncover customer behaviour patterns and deliver actionable business recommendations to boost sales and customer loyalty.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Dibs had accumulated large volumes of transaction data but lacked the analytical capability to extract meaningful insights. This project bridges that gap by transforming raw data into strategic recommendations for marketing and operations.

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

- **Data Cleaning** — handled missing values, corrected inconsistent entries, removed duplicates, standardised date formats
- **Outlier Detection** — box plots for age, quantity, and price per category
- **Customer Segmentation** — identified top-performing product categories (Clothing, Cosmetics, Food & Beverage)
- **Gender-Based Spending Analysis** — compared spending patterns across male and female customers
- **Age vs Spending Correlation** — scatter plot revealed a weak positive correlation
- **Payment Method Breakdown** — cash (44.8%), credit card (35.1%), debit card (20.1%)
- **Sales Trend Analysis** — monthly trends from 2021–2023 including seasonal patterns and anomaly detection

---

## 💡 Key Findings

- Clothing is the highest-demand category by quantity
- No significant gender difference in average spending
- Cash remains dominant but digital payments are growing
- Sales grew steadily through 2021 before a notable decline in March 2023
- Seasonal fluctuations suggest opportunities for event-driven marketing
